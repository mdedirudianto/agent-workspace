### Why the real-IP gap matters

Rekrut.Me rate-limits login (5 per IP per 15 min) and public applications (20 per IP per 15 min) in memory, keyed on the
client IP from `getClientIp()`. With no real-IP restore on `proxy`, every visitor arrives as a Cloudflare edge address, so
the limiters would throttle unrelated users together (or, with several edges, barely limit at all). The new vhost must
restore the real IP itself (`set_real_ip_from` Cloudflare ranges + `real_ip_header CF-Connecting-IP`, scoped to the
vhost, not global) and send `X-Real-IP`; the app is then run with `TRUSTED_IP_HEADER=x-real-ip`. Never trust the
left-most `X-Forwarded-For` hop.
