unchanged in the range) and restart-count baseline (operator stayed at 8 throughout).

Startup logs showed only the known-benign `@opentelemetry/api: Attempted duplicate registration`

real live traffic within seconds, workers logged `started 32 queue processors` — the new PII HMAC
boot-gate did not fire, confirming the env var was correctly present.
