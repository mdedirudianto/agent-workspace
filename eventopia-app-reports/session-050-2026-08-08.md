## Report

plus a broader complaint from organizer **Malang Creative Fusion** that "many" buyers weren't
getting their emails. Asked for read-only analysis only — via the platform's own API/DB state and
via Resend's own delivery log. No fix applied this session.

The paid order's `buyer_email` is **`mlninarhamadhani@gmail.com`** — the buyer's own typo on the

Our own record says the email was `sent` — that only means Resend's `POST /emails` returned 2xx.
Pulled the actual outcome from **Resend's own log** (`GET https://api.resend.com/emails`, prod

```json

```

**`last_event: "bounced"`** — Gmail hard-bounced it because `mlninarhamadhani@gmail.com` isn't a
real mailbox. WhatsApp delivery to the same order independently failed all 6 attempts
(`(#132001)`, the known unapproved-template gap — see `docs/launch/order-history-deploy.md`). Net
result: **this buyer got zero delivery on either channel**, even though their tickets are real and
`ISSUED` (`tkt_34191d45…`, `tkt_fa3cdd31…`).

The platform has no way to see this itself — there's no Resend webhook wired up, so
`tickets.email_delivery_status` and the order-history UI only ever reach `SENT` (API-accepted) and
never `BOUNCED`. A bounce is invisible to the organizer and to us unless someone checks Resend
directly, which is what this session did.

Two things fall out of this before even looking at delivery:

1. **281 orders expired unpaid vs. 180 paid** across the organizer's events — the majority of
   "orders" buyers start never actually complete payment. Some fraction of "I never got my ticket"
   reports are very likely buyers who never finished paying, not a delivery failure.
2. **Two near-duplicate live listings** for the same concert ("...Concert" and "...Concert by
   BRImo") split buyers, orders and support history across two `event_id`s — worth flagging to the
   organizer as bookkeeping/confusion risk independent of the email issue.

**Delivery, measured, not assumed** — across all 343 tickets issued to this organizer:

| Channel | SENT/delivered | FAILED | Stuck `ENQUEUED` |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp (`delivery_status`) | 4 | **275** | 64 (pre-migration-0124 tickets, never got a real status written back) |
| Email (`email_delivery_status`) | 337 | 0 | 0 |

Cross-checked all 167 "Mbois" e-ticket **email** sends against Resend's actual log (same
`GET /emails` pull, 200 most-recent records covered the full window back to 14 Jul since volume was
sparse before 06 Aug): **164 delivered, 3 bounced** — this buyer, plus `RSepta125@gmail.con`
(typo'd `.con` TLD) and `abdurrahmanasy3@gmail.com` (well-formed but bounced, cause not
determined). All three are buyer-entered addresses, not a platform or domain-reputation problem.

**Conclusion: WhatsApp, not email, is the dominant failure.** Only 4 of 343 tickets ever reached a
buyer over WhatsApp (the same accepted, documented `(#132001)` unapproved-template gap flagged in
`docs/launch/order-history-deploy.md`). Email succeeds ~98% of the time; the organizer's "many
people didn't get anything" complaints are overwhelmingly a WhatsApp problem wearing an email
costume, plus a handful of genuine buyer-typo bounces on top — this buyer's case is one of the
latter, compounded by also losing the WhatsApp channel.

### Fix path (not executed this session)

The resend endpoint already exists for exactly this: `POST /events-orders/{orderId}/resend` with
`{channel: 'email', to: '<corrected address>'}` — its own contract doc comment reads *"the
typo'd-email case, which is a large share of 'I never got it'"*. It sends to the override address
without writing it back onto the order. User confirmed the fix (resend to
`mlaninarhamadhani@gmail.com`) is being actioned on the deployment side; not run from this session.

## Findings summary

1. **Root cause for the reported buyer: their own typo on the paid order's email, confirmed
   bounced via Resend's API — not a platform delivery bug.** Their first (unpaid, correctly-spelled)
   order never had an email due in the first place.
2. **Platform has no bounce visibility.** No Resend webhook is wired up, so a hard bounce reads
   identically to a real delivery (`SENT`) everywhere in our own data — Resend's dashboard/API is
   currently the only source of truth for actual mailbox outcome.
3. **WhatsApp delivery is ~99% failed for this organizer** (4/343 tickets), a known/accepted gap
   (unapproved WABA templates), and is almost certainly the real driver behind "many emails missing"
   — buyers primarily expect WhatsApp in this market and read its absence as "nothing arrived."
4. **Two near-duplicate live event listings** for the same concert, splitting orders/analytics.
5. **281 expired-unpaid vs. 180 paid orders** for the organizer — a chunk of "I ordered but got
   nothing" complaints are likely buyers who never completed payment.

## Open follow-ups (new, session-050)

- **No Resend bounce/complaint webhook wired up.** `tickets.email_delivery_status` and the order
  drawer's "Email: Sent" only reflect Resend's initial API accept, never the actual mailbox
  outcome. Subscribing to Resend's `email.bounced`/`email.complained` webhooks and writing a
  `delivery.email.bounced` `order_event` would make this visible to organizers and support without
  needing an ad-hoc Resend API pull like this session did.
- **WhatsApp e-ticket delivery is still ~99% failing platform-wide for any organizer selling
  through this flow** (unapproved `eticket`/`eticket_link` templates) — already tracked as a
  standing follow-up since the order-history deploy; this session adds a concrete, large-scale
  data point (4/343 for one organizer) rather than a first-week guess.
- **Malang Creative Fusion has two live listings for one concert** — worth a proactive nudge to the
  organizer to unpublish/merge one, independent of the delivery issue.
