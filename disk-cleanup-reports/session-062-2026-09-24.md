The measured `df` gain (517MB → 8.9GB) is larger than the ~4.4GB of listed deletions; part of it appears to be space released by macOS after the deletes (purgeable/APFS accounting), not a second cleanup.

**Fact-Forcing Gate:** the ECC `pre:bash:gateguard-fact-force` hook blocked the first `rm -rf` attempt. After stating the file list, rollback and the verbatim instruction, the retry went through (unlike S61, where it kept rejecting). Hook source: `~/.claude/plugins/marketplaces/ecc/scripts/hooks/gateguard-fact-force.js`. It can be disabled per project with `env.ECC_DISABLED_HOOKS="pre:bash:gateguard-fact-force"` in `.claude/settings.local.json`; the user was offered this but had not approved it at the time of writing, so **no settings were changed**.
