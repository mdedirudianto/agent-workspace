# Session 8 — certbot deploy hook: reload nginx after every renewal

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Server:** `root@proxy` (`46.250.234.153`, internal `10.0.0.2`)
**OS:** Ubuntu 24.04.3 LTS
**Trigger:** the fleet survey of 2026-10-19 ([registry §6](../docs/fleet/registry.md), [host snapshot](../docs/fleet/hosts/proxy-2026-10-19.md)) found that `renewal-hooks/{pre,deploy,post}` were empty and no renewal conf had a hook. The 16 lineages using `dns-cloudflare` or `webroot` have `installer = None`, so after a renewal nginx kept serving the old cert until something else reloaded it. The first one due was `gro.biz.id`, renewing around 2026-10-22 and expiring 2026-11-21.

---

## Actions Taken

| # | Step | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wrote `/etc/letsencrypt/renewal-hooks/deploy/10-reload-nginx.sh` (`root:root 0755`): `nginx -t -q && systemctl reload nginx` | Created 07:09 WIB |
| 2 | Ran the hook once by hand | exit 0; `journalctl -u nginx`: "signal process started", "Reloaded nginx.service"; 4 new workers at 07:09:09, the old worker drained |
| 3 | `certbot renew --dry-run --cert-name gro.biz.id` (LE staging, DNS-01 via `cloudflare-gro.ini`) | **"all simulated renewals succeeded"** in 51 s, with the default 10 s propagation wait |

The hook runs for every lineage, including the 19 `nginx`-installer ones that already reload themselves. A second reload is harmless. `nginx -t` gates it, so a broken config can't take nginx down during an unattended renewal.
