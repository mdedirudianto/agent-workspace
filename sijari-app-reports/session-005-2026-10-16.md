## Findings & gotchas

- **Local commits ahead of the remote.** The deploy method clones the remote, so unpushed commits are invisible to it. Always compare `git rev-parse HEAD` with `git ls-remote origin main` before planning.
- **Auto-mode classifier blocks `git push`** in this workspace (denied here as "Data Exfiltration"). Run it as `! git push origin main`, or add a Bash permission rule.
- **Search engines may keep the old address** in cached snippets / structured-data until recrawled; nothing to do on our side.
- The hash comparison is easiest as `shasum -a 256` (macOS) vs `sha256sum` (server) on `sort`ed `./path` lists, after normalising the double space.
