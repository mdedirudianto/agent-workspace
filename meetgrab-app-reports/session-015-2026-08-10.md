## Pre-flight (what changed since last deploy)

19 commits, no migrations (still at `0006`), two independent workstreams:

1. **Summary failure chain fix** (`docs/MeetGrab_SummaryFailures_Deploy.md`) — the actual deploy
   target. Root cause: `LLM_ATTEMPT_TIMEOUT_MS` (hardcoded 30s) applied to **every** attempt including
   the last one in the primary→fallback chain, so a 90s total budget failed at ~60s with a third of
   the budget unspent — surfacing as the generic `"Couldn't generate the summary"` fallback message.
   Fix: the last attempt in the chain now takes whatever budget remains instead of another fixed 30s
   slice. Also adds a per-request cost ceiling (`SUMMARY_MAX_COST_USD`, default $0.50, enforced at the
   route and before each attempt), turns off Gemini fallback thinking (`thinkingBudget: 0`), and adds
   a `[summary] job failed: code=… stage=… cause=…` log line + GlitchTip capture where previously a
   failed job wrote nothing anywhere. Same commit also **inverts** `DAILY_SPEND_CAP_USD=0`'s meaning
   from "cap everything" to "unlimited" — the reason ordering matters for this deploy.
2. **Chrome Web Store listing + marketing video re-concept** (`store/`, `docs/`) — copy, screenshots,
   Remotion video scripts. No server-deployed code; out of scope for this session, confirmed via full
   unfiltered diff (`store/`, `docs/`, `CLAUDE.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` only outside `apps/`).

Confirmed against the live `app` server before touching anything: `git status --porcelain` empty,
pinned at `3d92282`, PM2 `meetgrab-api` 7-day uptime / 0 restarts, live `/v1/version` matched the
pinned SHA, `DAILY_SPEND_CAP_USD=20` (the safe, non-zero value — no live danger from the old
