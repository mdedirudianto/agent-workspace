## Incident — the order relay emptied `/var/www/grobiz/order` for ~10 s

- **What happened:** the first S4 attempt ran `rsync` with `-e 'ssh -n …'` to pull the release's `apps/order/dist` to the Mac. `ssh -n` breaks rsync's protocol, so the pull failed (`code 12`) and left an empty directory. The script had no stop-on-error.
- **Effect:** after the backup (`order.bak-20261016-033418`, `backup-identical`), `rsync -avz --delete` of that empty copy deleted `index.html` and `assets/`. `order.gro.biz.id` and `shop.gro.biz.id` answered **403 from 03:34:19 to 03:34:29 WIB**.
- **Fix:** I restored with `rsync -a --delete order.bak-20261016-033418/ order/` and `chown`. The tree md5 was back to `e759228e…` and both hosts served 200.
- **Retry:** with `set -euo pipefail`, no `-n` on rsync's ssh, and a refusal unless the copy is non-empty, holds `index.html` and its tree md5 equals the release's. The partner relay used the same guarded script.
- **Follow-up:** D122. Fix it together with D100 (automate the relay).
- Two identical 0.12.0 backups remain: `order.bak-20261016-033418` and `order.bak-20261016-033449`.
