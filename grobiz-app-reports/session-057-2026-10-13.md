## Findings

1. **`sw.js` is `public, max-age=14400` at the browser** (Cloudflare Browser TTL). Harmless (default `updateViaCache: imports`; edge revalidates) — grobiz follow-up D87.
2. **Public signed files are `public, max-age=86400, immutable`**: the browser HTTP cache serves a product photo offline across sign-out → sign-in for 24 h; the runbook row 10 expectation cannot hold — D88 (owner call).
3. **`origin/main` ≠ production** since 18:25 WIB (owner-intended). Never `git pull origin main` / `deploy.sh` on `app` until M2's own deploy — D89.
4. **Cart button `BAYAR Rp 10.000` on a PPN tenant** (payable Rp 11.100; payment sheet correct). Pre-existing — D90.
5. An early attempt to stage the old worker by routing the production origin in Playwright mixed a 0.96.5 worker with 0.96.4 pages in the test profile only; the profile was signed out (`/api/auth/logout` 200) and its site data cleared before the checks. Production was not affected.
