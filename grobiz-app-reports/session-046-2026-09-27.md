## 7. Post-deploy fixes (owner-approved, 11:57–12:07 WIB)

**Root cause (product bug, not data rot):** `PUT /api/settings` (`apps/api/src/routes/settings.ts` ~L367) lets an owner rename `business_name`, and writes **only Postgres**. Every ERPNext lookup uses `business_name` **as the ERPNext Company key** — `getDefaultWarehouse` (`company` filter), `posProfileName()` (`<name> POS`, till + dine-in finalize), Employee sync (`isOwnCompany`), company address. So any tenant that renames itself silently breaks all of them. (The 2026-09-19 20:37:13 `updated_at` on `dfd25d48` is a bulk touch of 18 tenants, not the rename itself.)
