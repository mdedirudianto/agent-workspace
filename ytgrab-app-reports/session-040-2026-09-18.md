## 5. Observations

- **Single-stream downloads take ~30–40 s to start.**
  - The 40 s was a cold download from each node with no process call first. Through the site after a process call it was 28.6 s, and the product canary took 26–29 s yesterday.
  - Before residential serves the file, the direct route hits `BotChallengeError`s (the missing `curl_cffi` from session-034).
  - The limiter isn't the cause: requests from a node's private address are exempt before any Redis call, and otherwise the limiter is capped at 200 ms.
  - This is the known slow-start item in repo FOLLOW_UPS.
- **The numa restart cut a visitor's download.** A 480p download of `tMIMERXR_os` (78.138.192.40) had streamed since 09:22 at ~100 KB/s. It ended at 234 MB, 3 s after the 10:01:05 restart. Its `download_events` row still reads `ok` with `completed_at` null and 0 bytes: rows are inserted as `ok` and updated at the end (since 2026-05-18). Both items are now in repo `docs/FOLLOW_UPS_SCRAPER_2026-09.md`:
  - drain a node at nginx before restarting it;
  - in-flight rows read `ok`.
